# Community Policy

This document is a planning placeholder for community-facing policies. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed before real event launch.

## Code Of Conduct Placeholder

ARF should publish a concise code of conduct before accepting event RSVPs.

Minimum expectations:

- Respect other attendees' boundaries, identities, privacy, and property.
- Follow organizer instructions during events.
- Do not share private event details, rental addresses, attendee information, or screenshots without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, stalk, or intentionally make others feel unsafe.
- Do not bring illegal substances, weapons, or unsafe behavior into ARF event spaces.
- Respect rental property rules and local laws.
- Report safety concerns to organizers promptly.

Organizer response options:

- Warning.
- Mediation or separation.
- Removal from event.
- Cancellation of RSVP or ticket.
- Removal from future ARF access.

## Privacy Notice Placeholder

ARF should explain that it uses Telegram identity to manage event access and stores profile, RSVP, preference, and survey data for event operations.

The notice should name the major processors:

- Telegram for chat, identity, and bot notifications.
- Stripe for payment processing if direct ARF-owned payments are added later.
- Cloudflare for DNS, tunnel ingress, access controls, and security.

## Payment And Refund Policy Placeholder

Annual retreat payment handling is deferred. Before paid registration opens, organizers should publish:

- What payment covers.
- Whether payments are deposits or full tickets.
- Refund deadline.
- Transfer policy.
- Waitlist/payment expiration rules.
- What happens if rental plans change or the event is cancelled.

## Mini Retreat Policy Placeholder

Mini retreats may be lower-formality local meets, but ARF should still publish:

- RSVP expectations.
- Capacity limits.
- Cancellation etiquette.
- Location privacy expectations.
- Organizer contact path.
